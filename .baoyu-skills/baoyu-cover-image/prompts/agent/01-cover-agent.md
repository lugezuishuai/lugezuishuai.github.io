# Composition
Create a top-down task-planning desk. A friendly AI assistant sits at the center with one goal card. Around it, arrange a clear circular sequence of large objects: a plan board, a hand using a tool, an observation magnifier, a reflection mirror, and an updated checklist. Add two small helper assistants passing task cards to show multi-agent collaboration. The cycle must read as “plan, act, observe, reflect, improve” without any written labels.

Use warm cream background, macaron pastel blocks, clean charcoal outlines, rounded flat-vector forms, subtle paper grain, soft shadows, and balanced whitespace.

Avoid robotic sci-fi bodies, neon loops, holographic interfaces, dark cyber themes, complex dashboards, readable text, letters, numbers, logos, or watermarks.
