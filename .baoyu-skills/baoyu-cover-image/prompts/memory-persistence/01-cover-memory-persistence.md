# Composition
Create a cozy archive-room metaphor. A friendly AI assistant files three visually distinct memory cards into a rounded cabinet: a fact card with a lightbulb, an event card with a calendar and photo, and a how-to card with a small checklist and tool. A curved path connects yesterday’s desk to today’s desk through the archive, clearly showing persistence across sessions. Include a small recycling basket for outdated memories to express selective forgetting.

Use warm cream, mint, lavender, sky blue, peach, coral accents, clean charcoal outlines, rounded flat-vector objects, light paper texture, and calm balanced spacing.

Avoid dark technology rooms, glowing databases, circuits, holograms, cyber motifs, readable text, letters, numbers, logos, or watermarks.
