# Composition
Create a friendly editorial flat-vector illustration on a warm cream background. Show one large central worktable where a simple rounded AI assistant character organizes several clearly recognizable inputs: conversation cards, a document folder, a memory notebook, a small toolbox, a calendar, and a magnifying glass. Colored paper paths connect these objects into one tidy output page leaving the desk. The visual metaphor must immediately communicate “assembling the right information for an AI task.”

Use rounded macaron pastel color blocks, clean uniform charcoal outlines, minimal 2D layering, subtle warm paper grain, soft shadows, and generous whitespace. Keep the composition simple, readable at thumbnail size, and educational rather than futuristic.

Do not use neon light, dark backgrounds, glowing circuits, holograms, cyberpunk motifs, dashboards, realistic humans, readable words, letters, numbers, logos, or watermarks.
